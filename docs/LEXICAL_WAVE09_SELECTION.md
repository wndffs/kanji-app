# Wave 09 Selection

Twenty exact pairs in `lexical-wave09-teaching.ts`: eight verbs, three i-adjectives and nine noun-class dictionary entries (some also used adverbially). Calendar nouns are not described as verbs simply because they modify an action.

The full verified local JMdict snapshot has one permitted exact spelling/reading identity and both RU/EN source senses for each selection. Parser commonness markers are 500 or 1000: positive dictionary-priority evidence only, not measured token frequencies, relative difficulty or modern JLPT certification. Full detailed observations remain outside Git.

Scope: communication (話す), resting/standing, understanding versus acquiring knowledge (分かる / 知る), using/making/returning, colours and quantity, reading/study and time/calendar routines. The wave completes useful deferred basics before claiming broader N3 coverage. Five new kanji precede these words; all other characters are already taught.

午前中 was considered but rejected: the selected local exact pair lacks RU source evidence. Shopping, work, health and travel topics remain reservations, not reviewed or published content. Genuine alternative pronunciations (e.g. 毎月・毎年) must be named as variants, not labelled invalid Japanese; this wave binds only its selected exact pairs.

Source evidence does not grant editorial approval. Publication requires independent review, all-registry preflight, a finite manifest and explicit apply/plan/publish.
